# Dispute-Safe Collections Agent
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Approved: Python 3.12 / LiveKit Agents, Next.js App Router / TypeScript, shadcn Radix, Vercel Hobby, LiveKit Build, Upstash Free.

## Users
Anonymous visitors exploring a personal, non-commercial fake-data invoice demo; technical reviewers inspecting tool evidence and evaluation provenance.

## Product Purpose
One shared text/voice agent answers scoped invoice questions, simulates disputes, pauses collections and exposes exact successful tool sources.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Exactly five tools. No real payment processing, email or human handoff. No paid provider calls authorized. All budgets zero; no live results or measured latency may be invented. Canary accounts never reach visitors. Provider-disabled state must remain honest and useful.

## Brand Commitments
Approved finance workspace: compact top bar, scenarios, bound invoice strip, 60/40 chat/evidence, mobile evidence sheet; cool slate plus restrained teal, Geist Sans/Mono, 6px radius, no decorative shadows, gradients, glass or emoji. No new aesthetic tournament.

## Evidence on Hand
Approved fixture and 30 fixed scripts in implementation plan. No provider credentials, deployment or measured evaluations at initialization.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard operation, labeled focus, 200% zoom, light/dark desktop/mobile, maintained axe checks; normal text contrast at least 4.5:1.
